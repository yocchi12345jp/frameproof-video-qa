# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must communicate respectfully, focus on technical merit, accept constructive feedback, and avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, publication of private information, deliberately disruptive behavior, and personal attacks are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.
